Core features

Milestone-based contract

Every milestone has a scope, deadline, acceptance criteria and value from day one, so there's no confusion later about what was agreed.

Documented delivery

Every file, link or reply to a revision request is logged with its date and author, building a clear record for each milestone.

Payment tied to acceptance

A milestone's value is only released once the client actually accepts it: no payment before the work, and no work without payment.

A record that holds up

If a dispute comes up, the full timeline of the agreement, deliveries and decisions is ready, instead of digging through scattered chats.

  • By when?
